🧠 Core Concepts

  • Scratch: Cue ball goes into a pocket or off table → opponent gets ball-in-hand.
  • Ball-in-hand: You can place the cue ball anywhere before shooting.
  • Slop shot: Ball goes in a pocket you DID NOT call → counts only if “slop allowed.”
  • Call shot: You must call ball + pocket before shooting.
  • Legal shot: Must hit your ball group first, or a rail after contact.
  • Break: First shot of game — spreads balls.

🏠 House Rules (Simple)

  • Call all shots (no slop)
  • Scratch = ball-in-hand
  • Scratch on 8 = loss
  • 8 must be called
  • Early 8 = loss

🏆 League Rules (APA Simplified)

  • Open break (no group assigned yet)
  • First called shot determines solids/stripes
  • Ball-in-hand after fouls
  • Defense allowed (safe play)
  • 8 must be called and clean

⚠️ Common Situations

  • Missed call → does not count (house rules)
  • Disagreement → replay or decide quickly
  • Combo shots allowed if final ball is called
